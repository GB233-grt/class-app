# Points Store User Rules

**Version:** 1.0  
**Applies to:** All Points Store account holders  
**Scope:** Student, teacher and administrator accounts and platform features

## 1. Accounts and passwords

1. Use only your own account. Do not lend, transfer or impersonate an account, or attempt to access or operate another person's account.
2. Keep your password secure and do not save your session on a shared device. If you suspect your password has been exposed or your account is compromised, contact a teacher or school administrator promptly.
3. When changing your password, use a new password that meets the requirements shown on the page and is not easy for others to guess.

## 2. Points and redemptions

1. Points are for school-approved learning incentives and rewards. They have no cash value and must not be sold, transferred or exchanged for cash.
2. Do not earn points or items through fabricated achievements, duplicate submissions, impersonation, altered requests or exploitation of system vulnerabilities.
3. Check the item, quantity and point cost before redeeming. Wait for teacher fulfilment for physical items. Digital items are for your own learning and must not be redistributed without permission.
4. Contact a teacher or administrator if you have a question about points, redemptions or fulfilment records. Do not attempt to alter data yourself.

## 3. Respectful use and content standards

1. Respect students and staff and follow school rules and classroom expectations.
2. Do not publish or distribute illegal, bullying, harassing, discriminatory, violent, sexual, privacy-infringing or otherwise inappropriate content.
3. Do not use the platform for commercial promotion, advertising, fundraising or external-event promotion unless approved by the school.
4. Campus announcements, including the Opening Announcement page, must be reviewed by an authorized person before publication. Report inaccurate, inappropriate or broken content to an administrator.

## 4. Security and regional permissions

1. Do not disrupt, scan or attack the platform, or bypass authentication, roles or regional permissions to access unauthorized data.
2. Use teacher and administrator features only within your duties and authorized regions. Do not view, export or share student information unrelated to your work.
3. If you discover a vulnerability, data issue, compromised account or unauthorized access, stop and report it to the school administrator. Do not exploit or publicly spread the issue.

## 5. Privacy and data use

1. The platform processes account and activity data needed for authentication, points accounting, redemption fulfilment, access control and security.
2. Do not collect, share screenshots of, or reuse another person's name, student ID, points or redemption history without authorization.
3. Data access follows role- and region-based permissions. Users may access only the information needed for their learning or work.

## 6. Violations

Depending on the circumstances, violations of these rules or school policies may result in a warning, reversal of improperly earned points or redemptions, account suspension or permission changes. Serious matters may be handled under school policies.

## 7. Updates

The school may update these rules as the platform and its operating requirements change. Material changes will be announced through the platform or school channels. Review and accept the applicable version before continuing to use the platform.

## 8. Contact

For account, points, content or security concerns, contact your teacher, homeroom teacher or the school-designated platform administrator.

---

These rules guide use of this school platform. They do not replace official school policies or applicable laws and regulations.
